About me

Animation is
performance.

I create character-driven animation where movement, emotion and story work together.

I am Jiayu Zhao, a 3D animation student at the Communication University of China, graduating in July 2028. Born in Budapest and based between Budapest and Beijing, I bring a cross-cultural perspective to visual storytelling and work in Chinese, English and Hungarian.

My focus is character animation—observing acting, refining body mechanics and shaping believable performances. Experience across modeling, rigging, texturing, lighting and post-production gives me an understanding of the wider 3D pipeline. I am seeking internships where I can contribute, learn and grow as a character animator.

Based between Beijing · Budapest
